## Score: 6.5 / 10
A big step up from v1 (4.5). The dither and palette now hold up next to the reference (clouds, sky, the sharp pixel look), the hook F-16 is recognisable, and the contact (20.7), operator and disconnect are readable. Captions and counters match the narration. But three of the ten beats still don't show what the narration says. "A long boom … little wings" (S6) shows empty sky and then a confusing striped tube. The "three tonnes a minute" x-ray (S8) looks like an abstract 2D icon on a tilted ghost. The "tanker cruises" establishing shot (S3) has a small KC-135 cut off by the edge. Add the tangency in S2 and the unphysical contrails in a weak ending, and on a phone it won't reach the reference channel's level yet.
