How do you refuel a fighter jet without *landing*?
You bring the gas station *with you*.
This tanker cruises at over *five hundred* kilometers an hour.
The fighter slides in, just meters *below its tail*.
In the back, an operator lies face down at a *window*.
They fly a long *boom*, steered by its own little wings.
The nozzle locks into a hatch on the fighter's *back*.
And the fuel pours in. Almost *three tonnes* a minute.
Minutes later, the jet drops away. *Full*.
The gas station? It just keeps *flying*.
